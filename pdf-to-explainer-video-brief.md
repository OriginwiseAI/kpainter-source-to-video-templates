## Beat plan

1. **Orient:** What problem or context does the PDF address?
2. **Focus:** Which claim, comparison, or process can the video make easier to follow?
3. **Show:** Which source figure, table, quote, diagram, or sequence supports it?
4. **Bound:** What does the source not prove or cover?
5. **Continue:** Where should the viewer return to the original PDF?
